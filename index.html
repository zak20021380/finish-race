How to play

  1. Move — tap one of your balls, then tap a glowing dot beside it. One step per turn; teams alternate and each may move any one of their balls.
  2. Wall — tap a grid line, then tap the ghost wall again to place it. Every turn, no limit.
  3. Legal only — a wall must leave every ball a path to the line, and can't share a stretch of line.
  4. Finish — the first ball of any team onto the checkered row wins.
