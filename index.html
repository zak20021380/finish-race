How to play

  1. Move — tap a glowing dot beside your ball. One step per turn.
  2. Wall — tap a grid line, then tap the ghost wall again to place it. Every turn, no limit.
  3. Legal only — a wall must leave both balls a path to the line, and can’t share a stretch of line.
  4. Finish — the first ball onto the checkered row wins.
