# 2026-10-15 — finish-race

## Critical UX fix: sticky ball selection + move-dot tap priority

The reported bug: picking a ball and then sliding the pointer towards a legal-move dot crossed a
groove, `previewWall` put the ball back down, and every dot vanished mid-travel.

- `src/boardmode.ts` — new `holdsBall(m)` predicate, plus the doc note on `previewWall` explaining
  *why* a hover must never reach it while a ball is in hand.
- `src/main.ts` `onMouse(p, click=false)` — returns early when `holdsBall(mode()) || ghost?.armed`.
  Hover previews walls only while nothing is in hand. Tapping empty board space still puts the ball
  down, which is the escape hatch that gives hover previews back.
- `src/render.ts` `hitTest` — new `hintCellAt()` containment test. A tap **inside a legal-move dot's
  own cell** now resolves to `move` at priority 1, and the groove is never even offered there. The
  old `spot.d <= MOVE_ZONE` (0.15 cell) test was radius-based and lost to the magnet whenever `reach`
  was narrower than half a cell — i.e. on every fat-cell board.
- Verified load-bearing by reverting each guard in turn: without the hover guard the dot ink drops
  from ≥70 to 42 (empty clay) and the click no longer moves; without the hit-test change the click
  arms a wall and the toast reads "Tap again to place".

## HUD redesign: two-row player cards + a tactical bomb key

- Flag moved off the sphere onto the name's line (`[flag] YOU ⚡ 5,800`). It used to be welded to the
  ball's corner, which clipped the blitz ring exactly when the clock mattered. `flagFor()` now falls
  back to `DEFAULT_FLAG = 'GB'` instead of handing `flagPhoto` a `null` (which paints an ISO chip
  reading "—" — the broken fallback in the screenshot).
- Card = `[.chip-ball: sphere + ring + seconds] [.chip-txt: .who-line(flag/name/XP) / .walls]`, and
  `#mine-btn` is now a **DOM child of `#chip-0`**, sitting at the right of the card in a duel.
- Dock grid: 2P `1.35fr 1fr` (the YOU card is wider because it carries the bomb); 3P/4P `1fr 1fr` +
  `padding-right: 60px`, with the bomb `position: absolute; top: 50%; right: 0` against `#chips`.
  Getting it out to the dock's column means `#chip-0` must stop being a containing block in 3P/4P
  (`position: static; transform: none`), so its turn ring moves from `.chip::after` onto its own
  box-shadow.
- Sizes tuned against a measurement, not a guess: 2P ball 36px, name 12px, flag 14px, XP 9.5px with
  the bolt carrying the unit (`.xp-u` stays in the DOM but is `display: none`, so the harness's
  `xpU === 'XP'` check still holds). Compact: ball 30px, name 10px, flag 13px, XP 9px, and `.wall-w`
  is hidden — the duel paints "8 WALLS", three and four seats paint the bare count.
- `.tmp/hud-probe.mjs` (new) dumps the dock's real geometry for 2P/3P/4P at 360x640 / 390x844 /
  430x932 — chip, ball, text column, flag, XP, ammo strip and bomb boxes, plus every overflow — and
  writes dock crops to `.shots/`. That is the tape the CSS was written against.
