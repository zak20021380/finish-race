## Corridor leaps, anti-oscillation, and 4-seat bot-backfilled rooms

### `src/rules.ts` — the leap, the step field, the ledger

- **Leap.** A ball may not walk into another ball but may LEAP over one, over ANY other ball (a
  team's own jams a corridor the same way, and in a 4-way "the opponent" is not one seat). Straight
  leap to the square behind the body when that square is open (`canStep` from the BODY's cell, not
  the mover's); otherwise a sidestep onto one of the body's two flanks when `canStep(body, perp)`
  holds. A body hemmed in on all three sides is a wall — nothing offered. `eachLeapTarget()` is the
  rule reported to a sink; `leapTargets()` is the array wrapper `rawSteps` uses. `rawSteps` is now
  the unfiltered move set and `steps()` is `rawSteps` + the anti-oscillation filter, so every door
  (tap, bot, blitz auto-move, wire) obeys it.
- **`stepField(s)`** — distance to FINISH in *actions* with the bodies standing, so a corridor two
  balls are wedged in is ONE action wide, not sealed. Reverse CSR graph (a sidestep is a one-way
  edge, so the plain forward BFS is wrong). Body cells are sources (a mover looks out from where it
  stands) but never targets. **Returns a shared scratch buffer — read it, don't hold it across
  another call.** It replaced the bot's `distFenced`.
- **Anti-oscillation.** `GameState.repeat = { seen: number[], streak }`, `REPEAT_LIMIT = 3`,
  `REPEAT_ADJUDICATE = 6`, `REPEAT_WINDOW = 16`. `boardFingerprint(s)` = FNV over turn + wall count
  + mine count + every ball cell. `noteBoard` files it in `endTurn`/`recordTurn`; a repeat climbs the
  streak. At the limit `steps()` narrows to strictly-forward steps; at twice the limit the race is
  ADJUDICATED to the nearest ball (`winner` + `adjudicated: true`). A `pass` is never filed (a
  hand-built pass loop would otherwise trip it). Real payoff: a sealed corridor whose far end is
  walled can't be leapt out of, and the ledger walks both balls back out and finishes the race.
- **Allocation discipline (the turn budget depends on it).** `blocks()` returns a shared `_blocks`
  pair of Sets — nothing may hold a `Blocks` across another `blocks()` call. `distField` reuses
  `_bfsQ`; `stepField` reuses `_revOff/_revPre/_stepDist/_stepQ/_body`. Without the scratch buffers
  the bot's p99 blew past 5ms; with them p50 ≈ 0.45ms, p99 ≈ 1.1–2.1ms. A single-sample max still
  spikes to ~3–15ms on a loaded box (GC/scheduler), which is the harness's `max < 15` assertion.

### `src/room.ts` (new) — lobby + RoomDriver

- Seats 2..4, each `human` or `bot`. `createRoom/seatHuman/backfill/startEarly/tick/waitLeft/
  takeover/reconnect`. `isAutomated` (badge + tier) and `isHuman` (may play a turn) are DELIBERATELY
  different questions: a vacant seat is neither. `rewardEligible` is false for every automated seat,
  so a bot — or a person who walked out of a seat the machine finished — claims no Star.
- `tierFor` counts automated seats BEFORE the index, which is what lets `backfill` hand out the
  `mixed` ladder (hard/normal/hard) seat by seat. A bot seat's recorded `difficulty` matches what it
  plays; `difficultyFor` is the one reader.
- `RoomDriver`: `submit(seat, action)` refuses `over|not_your_turn|automated|illegal`, applies the
  action and then plays EVERY automated seat due in the same call, so 1 human + 3 bots needs no
  second round trip and the human clock is never charged for the machine's. `createRoomDriver`
  backfills + launches its room, so a turn can never land on a vacant seat. `drop`/`resume` are the
  takeover and the reclaim. `BOT_TURN_BUDGET = 8`.

### `worker/matchmaker.ts` — rooms, additively

- `?room=<n>&bottier=<tier>` on `/ws/matchmake` makes a socket a room seat, NOT a queue entry (it is
  never pushed into the 1v1 queue). Full room → launch now; else the wait rides on
  `MatchmakerDO.alarm()` (`rearm()` sets the earliest deadline; `DurableObjectState.storage` is now
  optional in `worker/types.d.ts` because the harness has no storage backend). Frames added:
  `ROOM_FOUND` (matchId/seed/side/sizes/seats[{side,name,bot,difficulty}]), `ROOM_START`, `SEAT_BOT`.
  Moves/resigns reuse the 1v1 frames; `pushRoomEvents` skips the actor's own event. A dropped seat is
  `takeover` + `SEAT_BOT`, and the race continues. `status()` reports `players`/`bots`.
- 1v1 is untouched: the existing 34 matchmaking checks still pass verbatim.
