# 2026-10-31

## Post-match banter overhaul (banter.ts / banter.css / index.html / settings.ts)

- **Bubbles are now flex children of `.result-seat`**, not absolutes spanning the card. `width:
  fit-content; max-width: 68%; margin-inline-start: auto` → they park at the inline-end and can
  never cover the avatar/nameplate. The `.is-speaking > .result-id { opacity: .12 }` fade is gone.
- **`is-emote` mode**: a message that is only pictographs (`\p{Extended_Pictographic}` + joiners/
  VS/skin tones, no letters or digits) renders as a 42px circular glass medal with a spring
  `banter-emote-in` on the bubble and a `banter-emote-pulse` on the inner span.
- **Dock is frameless, two rows.** Row 1 = six 36px circular faces + a 36px composer toggle at the
  inline-end. Row 2 = `.banter-row-swap` grid where the chip rail and the composer share
  `grid-area: 1/1`, so the toggle swaps them **in place** (no third storey). `#banter-toggle`,
  `#banter-toggle-ico` (href swaps `#i-chat` ↔ `#i-close`), `#banter-close` are new ids.
- **`[hidden]` needs `!important`**: an author `display: grid|flex` outranks the UA `[hidden]` rule.
  `.result-say[hidden], .result-banter[hidden], .result-banter [hidden] { display:none!important }`
  — without it the dock rendered even for 3/4-seat parties and empty bubbles ate padding.
- Copy is 2–4 words per taunt in en + fa (`banterWin*`, `banterLose*`, `banterBot*`), plus new keys
  `banterComposeOpen` / `banterComposeClose`. ru/ar/es still fall back to en.
- Relay (`POST_MATCH_CHAT`), the 1.2s bot reply and `opponentLeft()` locking are untouched.

## Quick match locked behind a gold "COMING SOON" (gamemodes.ts / main.ts / style.css / index.html)

- **One switch**: `QUICK_LOCKED` in `gamemodes.ts`. `DEFAULT_SELECTION` becomes `bot` when set;
  `load()` maps any saved `duel_1v1|party_2v2` to `bot`; `setGameMode` bounces quick ids to `bot`
  (so the queue can never *become* the selection). `launchSelectedMode` refuses quick with a note
  + drawer instead of `queueMatch()` (queueMatch itself is still used by compete/arena).
- The quick row's `.gm-seg` + `.gm-filters` are now always shown (`showcase` in `paintSheetState`),
  not only while active — the locked pills ARE the teaser.
- `.gm-check` removed from the quick card only; the slot is `.gm-badge-soon` ("✨ SOON", gold glass,
  `gm-soon-glow` pulse). `.gm-lock` 22px coin prefixes the size strip. Inner tint is applied to
  `.gm-pick > :not(.gm-badge-soon)` at `.72`, pills at `.5` — never to the whole card, or the badge dims.
- Pills ship `aria-disabled`, **not** `disabled`: a tap must reach `bounceLockedCard` (toast
  "Online servers undergoing tuning • Live soon!" + `gm-shake` on the card). `.gm-soon` (2v2 corner
  chip) retinted cyan→gold so the card never mixes accents.
- `scripts/gm-sheet-check.js` still encodes the old contract (desc ≤ 44 chars, `open === active`,
  exactly one active row) — it will fail until it is rewritten for the locked card.
