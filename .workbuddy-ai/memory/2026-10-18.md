  `.chat-emote-art` and the bubble's `.chat-emote`. Only the art node animates, never the button,
  so `.chat-emote-btn.is-tapped` keeps its own transform.
- Keyframes in `chat.css`: `emote-bounce` (1.05s), `emote-rage` (.26s) + `emote-steam` on
  `.ec-steam` (`transform-box: fill-box`), `emote-flip` (1.5s, `transform-box: view-box`,
  origin `48.2px 40.4px`) + `emote-dart` on `.ec-eyes`.
- Bubble: `chat-bubble-pop` 200ms scale(.2)->1.15->1 with `transform-origin: 50% 100%` and **no
  fill-mode**, so the `is-out` transition (opacity 0, translateY(-10px)) can take the transform
  back. Emotes live 2.2s (`EMOTE_MS`), text 2.5s, exit 240ms. `setBubble` drops `show` + reflows
  to re-arm the pop on every send.
- Haptics: emote = `impact('medium')` then `notify('success')`.
- `verify-chat.mjs` now also asserts: motion class + running `animationName`/duration per pod,
  the bubble's own class/opacity/shape count, bubble foot above the chip top, the fade at 2.6s,
  and that `#board` does not shift when the sheet opens.
- **`verify-arena.mjs` is a pre-existing failure** (stale `.g-head`/chip selectors throw on null);
  confirmed identical with this change stashed. Not a regression.
