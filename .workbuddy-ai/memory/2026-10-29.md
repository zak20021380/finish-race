## Leaderboard "your rank" bar — overlap fix + 3-zone HUD

- **Root cause of the row-#5 overlap**: `.mind-podium`/`.mini-rank` were `flex:1 1 auto; min-height:0`
  while rows kept `min-height:40px`, so the band was allotted less than its content and the rows
  spilled out of their own box; the foot (later in tree order) painted over row 5. Nothing was
  `position:absolute`.
- **Fix**: rows are `flex: 1 1 0; min-height: 0; max-height: 64px` (scoped to the card) — they SHARE
  the band, so the band can never overflow. No row floors anywhere, including the media queries.
- Both bars are now one component: `.hud-mine` = `[hm-l badge/flag+name][hm-c pill][hm-r score]`,
  padding 8/12, r14, cyan→obsidian→amber glass gradient, cyan hairline border. Score right-inset is
  matched to the rows (rows carry `padding-right: 13px` = bar's 12px padding + 1px border).
- Hairline divider = `.slide-foot::before` (1px gradient, fades at both ends), 5-7px above the bar.
- Row hiding for short windows lowered to `max-height:560px`/`520px` (was 600/550) so the three
  required widths (320/360/390) always show all five rows.
- `home.ts`: `paintMindMine`/`paintNationMine` paint zones (never `textContent` on the bar — the
  `loadCountries` catch used to wipe the structure). i18n keys `hudYou|hudTop|hudUnranked|
  hudYourNation|hudPickNation` in all 5 languages; `onLang()` repaints both bars from `countryRows`
  and `mindFeed`.
- Compact mode `@media (max-height: 680px)` shrinks avatars to 22/19 because a 25-30px row slice
  cannot hold a 30px portrait. Compact blocks must set `padding-top/bottom` only — the shorthand
  resets the 13px right inset that aligns the score column.
